Bookmark Sync Settings

Note: Due to browser security restrictions, extensions cannot directly access local file paths.

To sync with local files (e.g., Google Drive), you'll need to:

1. Use the extension to export bookmarks (copy to clipboard)

2. Manually paste into your text files on Google Drive

3. To import, copy from your text files and paste into the extension
